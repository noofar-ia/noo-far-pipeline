# Dundal nag yiy ratt

## Rasion yiñ digal ñu lekk bis bu nekk

Ngir nag bu màgget, ni ñu koy seentu bis bu nekk mooy :
- **6 kg forage bu waw**
- **3 kg konsentrat**

Dañu wara méngale bariwaayu baayima bi ak diisaayu baayima bi, tolluwaayu yaram wi (ëmb, nàmp), ak jumtukaay yiñ am ci gox bi.

## Besoin yu amul benn werante ci diiru lactation

Nag buy nàmp moo gëna soxla dundu gu neex nag bu waw bi, rawatina lu jëm ci kàttan; defar meew dafay lekk xéewal yi rasion bi wara joxewaat—luko moy, defar bi dafay wàññeeku, njuram gi dafay wàññeeku (xoolal fiche "Reproduction"). Ci sistem yu yaatu yi ci Sahel, li gëna gàllankoor mooy ñàkkum ñax mu baax ci jamonoy taw; pexe yu ñuy yokk (jëfandikoo concentré, residu mbay mi, wala ñaxu cowpea) dañu wara njëkka bàyyi xel ci baayima yiy nàmp ci géttu bi yépp ngir mëna am njeexital yuñ mëna natt ci liggéey bi.

## forage bu am ci gox bi

Barina xeeti ñax yuñ digal ñu yokk ci regime bi, rawatina ci jamonoy taw yi:
- **Niébé** (sariit 58-74 ak 66-35) gàñcax gu am ñaari njeriñ (pepp + ñax), nangu nañu ko ngir defar ñax
- **Bourgou**
- **Résidu  mbay mi**: mburu céeb, mburu yu ndaw

## Dewormer naturel

Ci baykati famiy yu ndaw yi, dañuy faral di jëfandikoo xobi neem ngir ray gunóor—muy beneen pexe buy mottali pajum veterineer, rawatina ci barab yu bariwul ay produit farmasi.

## wàll bu am solo

Regime bu tolloowul (ñakk forage wala concentré) dafay indi jafe-jafe ci liggéeyum meew mi ak ci njurug nit—xoolal fiche bi tuddu « Reproduction » ngir xam lëkkaloo gi am ci diggante diisaay bi, at yi, ak njurug baayima yi.
